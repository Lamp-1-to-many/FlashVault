About & User Guide

How FlashVault works, Easy / Good / Hard difficulty rules, formatting syntax, backups, and version history.

About FlashVault

FlashVault is a single-file, offline-capable flashcard web app built for desktop and mobile browsers (including GitHub Pages). Once loaded online, it caches itself and persists your data locally in IndexedDB until you clear browsing data.

  • Bilingual Support: Generous line height and uniform button heights keep English and हिन्दी (Devanagari) text aligned without clipping.
  • Daily Study Streak & Goal: Track consecutive study days and your daily card target directly on the Home dashboard.
  • Large Local Storage: Incremental IndexedDB storage supports thousands of cards and high-resolution images beyond the 5 MB localStorage limit.
  • Zero External Dependencies: Works completely offline with built-in MathML, code blocks, and rich text formatting.
  • Smart Back Navigation: Browser Back, mobile swipe Back, and desktop Backspace return to your previous screen.

How Flashcards Work (Easy, Good & Hard)

In Practice mode, pick a subject, select one or more topics, and choose how many cards to study. Each card stays sized to the taller side so buttons never jump when you flip.

  • Show Answer / Next Card: Reveal the back of the card, or skip ahead to the next card without changing its difficulty rating.
  • Hard (1-Day Interval): Marks the card for frequent review, places it in the Hard focus group on Home, and automatically re-queues it within the current session when Retry Hard cards is enabled.
  • Good (3-Day Interval): Marks steady recall and schedules the next review in 3 days.
  • Easy (7-Day Interval): Marks mastered cards and schedules the next review in 7 days.

CSV & JSON Import, Export & Tags

Manage large decks easily from the Cards Bank page:

  • CSV Header: subject,topic,subtopic,front,back,tags (UTF-8 for हिन्दी; wrap commas/newlines in quotes "..."; separate tags with ;).
  • JSON & Export: Import JSON arrays or export filtered/all cards to plain CSV (with UTF-8 BOM) or JSON anytime.
  • Tag Filtering & Duplicate Protection: Filter by #tags in Bank & Practice, and automatically skip duplicate cards on import or warn when adding duplicates.

Encrypted Backup & Offline Persistence

  • Encrypted Backup: Uses Web Crypto API (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM 256-bit) to lock your cards, images, and Easy/Good/Hard progress into a passphrase-protected .fvault.json file.
  • Restore Options: Merge a backup with your current bank or replace the entire bank cleanly.
  • Offline Persistence & Service Worker: Requests persistent browser storage (navigator.storage.persist()), caches the app shell, and registers companion sw.js (place sw.js next to index.html on GitHub Pages) for full offline reloads until browsing data is cleared.
